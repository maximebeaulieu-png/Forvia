Generic pill label — severity chips, insurer ratings, page-language badges, counts. For a certificate verdict use `DecisionChip` instead.

```jsx
<Badge tone="red" icon={<Icon name="ban" size={12} />}>BLOCK</Badge>
<Badge tone="ink" mono>A+ · AM Best</Badge>
<Badge tone="neutral" mono>fr</Badge>
```

`variant="outline"` is reserved for formal defects, matching the outline treatment of the *Not admissible · resubmit* chip.
