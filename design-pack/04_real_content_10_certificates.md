Machine-readable version: `data/samples/ground_truth.json` (generated by `tools/build_ground_truth.py`, which also implements the scoring formula of `docs/04_scoring_rules.md`). Page images and text layers: `data/samples/<id>/pages/`.

**Reference dates.** Each certificate is analysed at its `receivedAt` date (issue date + 7 days, simulated). The portfolio "today" for the demo is the `demoClock` = **2025-04-15**. FX rates are indicative ECB rates at reception; live mode fetches real ones.

**Reading the expert verdicts.** Richard Mekouar reviewed most files live on 17/08/2026. Quotes are kept in French in the JSON. Where he did not comment a file individually, the verdict is *inferred* from his stated rules and flagged `reviewedInSession: false`.

### The three mismatches are one question
All three (08, 09, 10) are "stamp missing, everything else fine". Richard's written rule (Q05: *absence de tampon = alerte bloquante, pas de score*) and his live behaviour on sample 08 (*"l'acheteur doit renvoyer l'attestation : merci de mettre le tampon"*) lead to the **same action** — ask the supplier for a corrected certificate — but a different **label**. Design decision:

- The rules engine keeps the strict gate (auditable, matches the written answer).
- The UI labels `NO_GO / FORMAL_DEFECT` as **"Not admissible — resubmission required"** with a secondary line *"Coverage analysis suggests the certificate would be [acceptable / insufficient] once corrected"*, so buyers send **one** request that covers both the formality and the coverage gaps.
- The Requirements Profile exposes `stampMissingSeverity: BLOCK | REQUEST_CHANGES` (default BLOCK) → to be calibrated with FORVIA in the scoring workshop (Q44).

## Annotation conventions (for anyone extending the set to 100–200 certificates)

- One JSON object per certificate, same shape as in `ground_truth.json`. Required: `documentType`, `issuer.type`, `policyNumber`, `period`, `visual.stamp/signature`, normalized `guarantees[]` with `amountOriginal/currency/basis/status/page`, `exclusions[]`, `gates`, `expert.verdict`.
- `status` values: `COMPLIANT | BELOW_MINIMUM | MISSING | COVERED_NO_AMOUNT | EXCLUDED | UNCLEAR | PRESENT` (PRESENT = secondary guarantee found, no threshold).
- Always record the **page** and, when possible, a short verbatim `labelOriginal` — the eval harness checks that the extractor's source quote overlaps it.
- Expert quotes in original language; never paraphrase a verdict.
- Keep `receivedAt` realistic (≥ issue date). Expiry logic depends on it.

## Known limitations of this seed set
- 10 documents, 5 languages, 4 currencies — enough to build and demo, not to claim ≥ 90 % accuracy. The POC contract foresees 100–200 annotated certificates (Q02: annotation ownership still open; Richard offers a training session).
- No US ACORD 25 form, no Chinese/Arabic certificate, no captive, no multi-insurer tower. Add at least one of each before the performance report.
- Richard's per-file comments were sometimes made while switching files; attributions marked "probable" in the JSON should be confirmed with him in 10 minutes.
